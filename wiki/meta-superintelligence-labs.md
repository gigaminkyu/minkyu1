# Meta Superintelligence Labs

Meta's reorganized AI division (MSL), led by Chief AI Officer **Alexandr
Wang**. Wang arrived through Meta's **$14B Scale AI deal**. MSL makes the
**Muse** model family, which replaced the open-weight Llama line as Meta's
flagship.

Timeline:
- **2025-04**: Llama 4 Maverick, Meta's last frontier model before the Muse
  family.
- **2025-11**: Yann LeCun, longtime Chief AI Scientist and open-source
  advocate, leaves Meta amid the turn toward closed models.
- **2026-04-08**: [[Muse Spark]], MSL's first model, released with closed
  weights.
- **2026-07-09**: Muse Spark 1.1.
- **2026-08-10**: [[Muse Glimmer]], a partial return to open weights.
- **2026-09-08**: the [[Meta Muse]] agent launches.
- **2026-09-23**: Connect. [[Muse Charm]], Mac app control, new connectors.
