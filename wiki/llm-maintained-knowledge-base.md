# LLM-Maintained Knowledge Base

A personal knowledge system where the human curates sources and asks questions
while the model does the maintenance — filing, cross-referencing, summarizing,
and reconciling. The premise: most knowledge systems die of *maintenance*, not
of bad ideas. Collecting is easy; keeping fifty interlinked notes current is the
work nobody does twice. Moving that work to the model is what makes the system
survive.

The throughline: the human owns judgment and the raw record; the model owns the
bookkeeping; the wiki is a compiled artifact that **compounds** rather than a
pile that grows. The division of labor is enforced by [[The Three Layers]], the
compounding by [[Compile Not Retrieve]], the growth by the [[Ingest Workflow]],
and the durability by [[Linting the Wiki]].
