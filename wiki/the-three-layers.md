# The Three Layers

The system is three layers with three owners, which must never be blurred
(Rules 1 and 2):

| Layer   | Path        | Owner | Mutable?                         |
| ------- | ----------- | ----- | -------------------------------- |
| Sources | `raw/`      | Human | No — immutable, append only      |
| Wiki    | `wiki/`     | Model | Yes — regenerated by the model   |
| Schema  | `CLAUDE.md` | Both  | Yes — by agreement               |

**Sources are immutable** (Rule 1): everything saved lands in `raw/` and is never
edited afterward. If a source is wrong, add a *correcting source* rather than
rewrite history — editing raw by hand creates two systems of record with no way
to tell which is true.

**The model owns the wiki** (Rule 3): the human chooses what enters `raw/`, asks
questions, and thinks; the model summarizes, cross-references, files under the
right entity, and updates neighbors. If the human is doing the bookkeeping, the
schema is underspecified, not the model.

**The schema is shared**: `CLAUDE.md` (or `AGENTS.md`) holds the rules and
belongs to both. The boundaries are what make the system trustworthy — they are
gone the moment the model writes into `raw/` or the human hand-tunes `wiki/` to
win an argument.
