# Muse Charm

A keychain-sized device built for [[Meta Muse]], unveiled at **Meta Connect
2026-09-23**. It has a 2-inch OLED screen, a front camera and a built-in 5G
modem, with a "quasi-Tamagotchi" personality. Meta says it carries "the whole
real-time voice and avatar stack" of Muse.

- Zuckerberg says it is planned to ship for the December holidays. Price
  **not announced**.
- It was revealed about a week before OpenAI's expected consumer-device event.
  CNBC called it a head start for Meta, "but Zuckerberg's strategy remains
  unproven."
- The same keynote introduced $1,299 Meta VR Glasses. Muse is also coming to
  Meta's AI glasses.
- The privacy critique of always-on camera and microphone devices applies
  here too; see [[Meta Muse]].
